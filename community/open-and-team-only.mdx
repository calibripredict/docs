---
title: "Open and Team only"
description: "Every community market is public. The Open or Team only setting controls one thing — who may add markets to an event — and with it which proof types an outsider may use and who posts each market's result."
---

**Every community market is publicly listed, and anyone can trade it and dispute it.**
An event's **Open** or **Team only** setting controls one thing: **who may add markets
to it.** It never makes a market private.

## Who may add markets

| | Calibri event | Community event, Open | Community event, Team only |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example | "Red Sox vs Yankees" | "Will the Main Rd pothole be fixed?" | "Saturday golf round" |
| Who may add markets | Any member | Any member | The creator and the members who joined their team from the invite link |
| Proof types an outsider may use | Only proof anyone can find | Only proof anyone can find | Outsiders cannot add |
| Community markets per event | 50 | 20, the creator's team included | 20 |
| Per member per event | 5, under one bond | 5, under one bond | 5, under one bond for each member who adds |

**Your team** is you and every member who joined it from your invite link, up to 10.
See [Your team](/community/creating-a-market#your-team).

### Which setting an event has

| Event | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| A Calibri event | Always **Open** |
| A community event whose proof anyone can find — a news or web link, an official source or a published document | Always **Open** |
| A community event proved by a participant photo or participant document | **The creator chooses.** The default is **Team only** |

The choice is made in step 2 of the [wizard](/community/creating-a-market#who-can-add-markets), at creation. **It cannot be changed once the event is live**, like everything else about it.

## The proof restriction

On a Calibri event, and on any Open community event, a member who is not on the
event's team may add markets **only with proof anyone can find**: a news or web link,
an official source or a published document. Participant photos and participant
documents stay with the creator's team, because only the people who were there can
produce them.

> Thabo's golf event uses participant-photo proof, and he created it as Open. Pieter,
> who is not on Thabo's team, adds "Will the club post a course-record notice?" with published-document
> proof — allowed. Had Pieter chosen participant photo, it would be refused.

A member who is not allowed to add markets to an event sees no **Add a market**
button on it. They can still view, trade and dispute every market in it.

## Who posts the result

Only a team may post a result. Who that team is depends on the event's setting.

| Event | Who posts |
| --- | --- |
| **Team only** | The **event's team** — its creator and the members who joined from the invite link — posts the results for every market in the event, whoever added it |
| **Open**, including every Calibri event | **Each member who added markets posts their own**, with their own team. The event creator's team posts only its own markets |

Nobody else can post: not other holders and not other members. Posting windows,
disputes, what happens if a team stays silent, and the exceptional cases where
Calibri steps in are in [Resolution and disputes](/community/resolution-and-disputes).

**In both cases, the member who added a market pays for it and earns from it.** They
pay their own check fee and — for their first market in the event — their own bond,
and they earn the creator reward on that market.

> On "Red Sox vs Yankees", Lee adds two markets and Sam adds one. Lee's team posts
> Lee's two results; Sam's team posts Sam's. Each has their own bond, team and reward,
> and a dispute on Sam's market leaves Lee's and Calibri's markets untouched.

> On Thabo's Team only golf event, Zanele — who joined Thabo's team from his invite link — adds "Will Player C
> break 90?". She pays her own 1.50 USDC and earns the creator reward on her market.
> The event's team posts its result, as for every market in a Team only event.

## Rules that apply to every added market

- **Relevance.** A market added to an existing event must be about it and decided by it, or within its time. The wizard asks how it relates, and the content check gives a verdict. See [Relevance](/community/creating-a-market#relevance).
- **Mature must match.** An added market must match the event's Mature flag, or it is refused.
- **Named people.** The [naming rules](/community/terms#named-people) apply to each market: name a Calibri member with a placeholder such as "Player 1".
- **Close time.** An added market must close no later than the event.
- **Caps.** When an event is full you see **This event is full** and are sent to its existing markets. Nothing is paid.
- **Every community rule applies per market**: the 8% taker fee, no maker rebates or liquidity rewards, disputes, evidence, the creator reward and the deadlines.
