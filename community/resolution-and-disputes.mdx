title: "Resolution and disputes"
description: "How a community market is decided — the creator's team posts the result with proof, a 30-minute dispute window follows, and a disputed result goes to a panel of three AI models whose ruling is final. What happens if nobody posts, every outcome, and the few cases where Calibri steps in."
---

**The creator's team posts the result with proof. Every posted result then runs a
30-minute dispute window, and if nobody disputes, it is final.** A dispute is decided
by a panel of three AI models from different vendors, with evidence open to every
trader in the market, and **its ruling is final at every market size.** There is no
appeal to UMA or to any other arbiter.

Community markets settle through the same on-chain path as Calibri's own markets:
Calibri reports the outcome with its operator key, and winning contracts become
redeemable for 1.00 USDC. See [Market resolution](/non-custodial/resolution).

## What happens, in order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trading closes">
    At the time the creator set — never earlier. Until then the market trades, including while the event is in play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The team posts the result">
    With evidence of the declared proof type, from the close until 24 hours after **Result expected by**. The first post counts. A result must be posted even if nobody traded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The AI watcher checks it">
    If the result looks wrong, a public flag appears on the market, every holder gets a push notification, and the dispute window is extended once, by 30 minutes. The watcher never disputes on its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="30-minute dispute window">
    Anyone who traded the market or holds a position in it may dispute, with evidence. No dispute: the market is final when the window ends. There is no "accept result" step and no early finality.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evidence, rebuttal, ruling">
    If disputed: every trader in the market is notified and may add evidence to either side during the evidence window. Then one rebuttal each (1 hour), then the AI panel rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Final">
    Winning contracts redeem in full. A winning disputer is paid, and the creator reward is settled, with the next daily payout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Posting the result

| | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Who | The team. For a [Team only](/community/open-and-team-only) event, the event's team posts every market; on an Open event or a Calibri event, each member who added markets posts their own, with their own team |
| When | One window: from the close until **Result expected by + 24 hours** |
| What | The result, with evidence of the declared proof type |
| Which post counts | The first one |
| Cost | Nothing. The bond paid at creation covers whoever on the team posts |
| Creator reward | Stays with the creator, whoever on the team posts |

Nobody outside the team can post. Posting requires the
[public-and-permanent acknowledgement](/community/evidence-and-privacy#public-and-permanent).

## If the creator's team does not post

**A silent creator cannot force a void.** When the posting window ends with no
result, the traders answer instead.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Every holder is notified">
    "The creator didn't post a result. Submit the answer with evidence."
  </Step>
  <Step title="24-hour answer window">
    Any member who currently holds a position may submit an answer with evidence of the declared proof type — custodial members included. It is free, within the [upload limits](/community/evidence-and-privacy#limits).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The AI panel decides">
    From the submitted answers. Where the proof is something anyone can find — a news or web link, an official source or a published document — the panel also reads the declared source itself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The normal 30-minute dispute window">
    Then final, or a dispute as usual.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The market voids at 0.50 only if **nobody answers, or the panel cannot tell.**

For the creator, an abandoned event means the **creator reward is forfeited**, the
abandonment goes on their [creator score](/concepts/community-creator-score), and
**two abandonments within 30 days bring a 30-day creation ban.**

## Disputing a result

### Who may dispute

You may dispute a market's posted result if you have a self-custodial wallet **and**
either:

- you have **traded in that market** — at least one fill, even if you have since sold; or
- you **currently hold a position** in it.

The app enforces this. There is **one dispute per market**. A member on a custodial
balance cannot dispute, but may add evidence to someone else's dispute.

### Filing a dispute

- **Evidence is required when you file.** A dispute with nothing attached is refused, nothing is paid, and the slot stays open.
- **You pay the dispute fee and a dispute bond** in one payment, at one signing prompt. Both amounts are shown before you confirm, with the fact that neither comes back if the dispute fails.
- **Starting a dispute claims the slot at once.** Your payment must confirm **within 10 minutes**, or the claim lapses and the slot reopens for anyone; nothing is charged. A payment that confirms after the claim lapsed, or after the market is final, is paid back to you in full, fee included, with the next daily payout.
- **You must also tick the [public-and-permanent acknowledgement](/community/evidence-and-privacy#public-and-permanent).** Your dispute statement is public.

### Fee, bond and windows

The dispute window is 30 minutes at every size. The evidence window and the dispute
bond follow the **disputed market's own open interest, measured when the market
closed** — not the event's.

| Disputed market's open interest | Under 250 USDC | 250 to 2,500 USDC | Over 2,500 USDC |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dispute window after a result is posted | 30 min | 30 min | 30 min |
| Extension when the watcher flags (once) | + 30 min | + 30 min | + 30 min |
| Evidence window after a dispute is filed | 6h | 12h | 24h |
| Rebuttal, each side | 1h | 1h | 1h |
| Dispute fee | 0.60 USDC | 0.60 USDC | 0.60 USDC |
| Dispute bond | 10 USDC | 10 USDC | 50 USDC |
| **You pay** | **10.60 USDC** | **10.60 USDC** | **50.60 USDC** |

Every window runs its stated length **at any hour**. There is no night rule.
Deadlines are acted on within a few minutes of passing, not to the second.

<Warning>
  **The fee and the bond are payments to Calibri, not deposits.** Calibri holds nothing on your behalf. If you win, Calibri pays you back everything you paid, plus the creator reward, as a reward. If you lose, nothing comes back. See [Fees, bonds and rewards](/community/fees-bonds-and-rewards#disputing).
</Warning>

### Open evidence

When a dispute opens, **every member who traded in that market is notified** and may
add evidence to either side during the evidence window. It is free and needs no bond.
Caps per person and per side, labels on every item and the treatment of linked
accounts stop one side winning by volume. See
[Evidence in a dispute](/community/evidence-and-privacy#evidence-in-a-dispute).

### Losing disputes repeatedly

A member who **loses 3 disputes in 30 days** cannot dispute for 30 days. It shows on
their record; they can still trade and add evidence to others' disputes. An "unsure"
ruling does not count as a loss.

## The AI watcher

- It checks **every** posted result against the declared proof type and the market rules.
- It can only **flag**. A flag is public on the market and pushed to every holder.
- A flag extends the dispute window **once**, by 30 minutes.

## The AI panel

- **Three models from different vendors** decide independently, on the same sealed inputs. The two positions are labelled **A** and **B**, not "creator" and "disputer".
- **The decision is a 2-of-3 majority** in which each agreeing model is at least **0.70** confident. **It is final.**
- **Weak proof is the only dispute void.** If the declared proof is missing, unreadable or ambiguous, the market voids and the disputer wins.
- **Panel unsure.** Below the threshold, or a 1-1-1 split, the panel runs once more. If it is still unsure, **the posted result stands.** An unsure panel does not void the market, does not count as a loss for the disputer, and is not passed to staff.
- **In public.** The prompts and the pinned model versions are published on [The AI panel](/community/ai-panel). Each case's full log — inputs, every model's answer and reasoning, and the parties' statements — is linked from the market's resolution panel on calibri.io and stored permanently.

How the models are asked, what they see and how hidden instructions in evidence are
handled: [The AI panel](/community/ai-panel).

## Every outcome

| How it ends | The market | The disputer | The creator reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No dispute | Final on the posted result | No dispute | Paid to the creator |
| Dispute, result upheld | Final on the posted result | **Loses.** Nothing back | Paid to the creator |
| Dispute, result overturned | Final on the panel's answer | **Wins.** Everything they paid back, plus the creator reward | Paid to the disputer |
| Dispute, declared proof missing, unreadable or ambiguous | **Void** at 0.50 | **Wins**, as above | Paid to the disputer |
| Dispute, panel still unsure after the re-run | Final on the posted result | Bond back; the 0.60 USDC fee is kept | Paid to the creator |
| The market's own "Void if…" rule applies | **Void** at 0.50 | If there was a dispute: bond back, fee kept | Paid to the creator |
| Team did not post; holders answered and the panel decided | Final on the panel's answer, after its own dispute window | If disputed: a winner gets fee and bond back; otherwise as for any dispute | Forfeited — paid to nobody, even if a disputer wins |
| Team did not post; nobody answered, or the panel cannot tell | **Void** at 0.50 | No dispute | Forfeited |
| Policy takedown | **Void** at 0.50 | If there was a dispute: bond back, fee kept | Forfeited |
| Admin override (below) | Void or a result | Bond back, fee kept | Paid to the creator, unless the override is for the creator's own fraud |

**A void pays 0.50 per contract to both YES and NO.** It is not a refund of what you
paid: a member who bought YES at 0.80 loses 0.30 per contract on a void; one who bought
NO at 0.20 gains 0.30.

The creator's 1 USDC bond is never paid back once the event is live, in any of these
cases.

## Admin override: exceptional cases only

The policy is one sentence: **"We do not decide community outcomes, except in these
exceptional cases."**

An admin may void a market or decide a dispute only when one of these applies:

| Case | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Court order or legal requirement | An order to stop or unwind a market |
| Illegal-content takedown | A market or its evidence breaks the law |
| Proven fraud or exploit | Fabricated proof established beyond the dispute process; a bug used to take funds |
| AI, vendor or system failure | The panel cannot run; a vendor outage; a platform fault that corrupts a ruling |

- **Disagreeing with a panel ruling is not on the list.** A wrong-looking ruling stands.
- **Only before the outcome is on-chain.** An override can act only before the outcome is reported on the blockchain. Once reported, the outcome is final and cannot be reversed.
- **Shown on the market.** Every override appears on the market or event itself: **Resolved by Calibri admin**, the action (void or result), the reason and the date. No staff names.
- **Money.** A disputer gets the bond back and the fee is kept. The creator keeps the creator reward — unless the override is for the creator's own fraud, which forfeits it and brings a permanent creation ban.

## If Calibri fails to report

Every community market is registered with the same on-chain resolver as Calibri's
own markets, so the same **safety net** applies: if Calibri has not reported an
outcome by the market's registered deadline, anyone can escalate it to UMA, and past
a later hard cap anyone can force a void at 0.50 per contract. See
[Market resolution](/non-custodial/resolution#the-escalation-ladder).

This is a fallback for **Calibri going silent**, not a way to appeal a ruling. A
dispute is decided by the AI panel, and its ruling is final.

## Timers

| Timer | Runs | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trading closes | At the set time only | The book freezes |
| Result expected by | The close, or up to 7 days later | Starts the 24-hour countdown to the posting deadline |
| Posting window | Close to Result expected by + 24h | The team may post. The first post counts |
| Answer window | 24h after the posting window, only if nobody posted | Any holder may answer with evidence; then the panel decides |
| Dispute window | 30 min from a posted result, or from the panel's decision on answers | No dispute: final |
| Watcher extension | Once, + 30 min | Added to the dispute window when the watcher flags |
| Evidence window | 6h / 12h / 24h by open interest | Every trader in the market may add evidence |
| Rebuttal | 1h | One each; then the panel rules |
| Payouts | Daily | Creator reward and winning-disputer payouts within 24h of the market becoming final |

### Time from posting to final

| Case | Time |
| --- | --- |
| No dispute | 30 minutes after the result is posted |
| No dispute, watcher flags | 1 hour after the result is posted |
| Disputed, under 250 USDC | About 7.5 hours after the dispute |
| Disputed, 250 to 2,500 USDC | About 13.5 hours after the dispute |
| Disputed, over 2,500 USDC | About 25.5 hours after the dispute |
| Team does not post, holders answer, no dispute | About 48.5 hours after Result expected by |
| Worst case | About 75 hours after Result expected by |

A dispute on one market delays only that market. The other markets in the event —
the creator's and Calibri's — become final on their own timelines.

## Worked example

"Will the Main Rd pothole be fixed by Fri 18:00?", proof type news or web link, with
the local news site and the council's notice page named as sources.

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Fri 18:00 | Trading closes. Open interest is 1,700 USDC |
| Fri 18:20 | The creator, who holds NO, posts NO, citing an article from before the repair |
| Fri 18:20 | The watcher flags it: the article predates the deadline. Every holder is notified; the window is extended to 19:20 |
| Fri 19:05 | A YES holder disputes with the council's "completed" notice dated Thursday, paying 10.60 USDC. The 12-hour evidence window runs to Sat 07:05 |
| Overnight | Two other YES holders add their own dated photos of the repaired road |
| Sat 07:05–08:05 | One rebuttal each |
| Sat about 08:20 | The panel rules YES, 3 of 3. The result is overturned. Final |

The disputer is paid **18.60 USDC**: their 10.60 back plus the 8 USDC creator reward.
YES holders redeem in full. The overturn goes on the creator's record. Without the
watcher's extension, the dispute at 19:05 would have been 15 minutes too late.
