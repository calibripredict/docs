Creating a community event takes **four screens and one signing prompt**. Each screen
asks one question, offers a template and explains itself in a line of helper text.
There is no gate: no KYC, no account age, no trading history.

**One condition: you need a self-custodial wallet.** You pay and predict from your own
[Safe](/non-custodial/the-safe). A member on a custodial balance cannot create a
community market or dispute one, but can still trade, add evidence to a dispute and
answer when a creator stays silent.

| Step | The question on screen | What you provide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What are people predicting? | Event name, up to 5 yes/no questions, category |
| 2 | How will we know the answer? | Proof type and source, when trading closes, the YES rule for each question (we write a first version for you). The rarer settings are under **More options** |
| 3 | Review and sign | Your first prediction (YES at 50c for 1 USDC unless you change it), then one signing prompt |

## Two ways in

- **Create a community event** — a new event of your own, with up to five questions.
- **Add a market to this event** — from a Calibri event page, or from a member's event that lets you. The event name and category are already set, so step 1 asks only for your questions, plus [one line on how they relate](#relevance) to the event.

The first market you add to an event costs the check fee and the bond. Each further
market you add to the same event costs another check fee and its own first prediction, with
**no second bond**. See [Fees, bonds and rewards](/community/fees-bonds-and-rewards).

## Step 1: What are people predicting?

```
Step 1 of 3 · What are people predicting?

Start from a template
  [ parkrun time ] [ 5-a-side with friends ] [ Local issue ] [ Sports match ]

Event name
  [ Thursday 5-a-side                                ]
  Name the occasion. The questions come next.

Yes/no questions                                2 of 5
  1 [ Will the Bibs beat the Skins on Thursday?    ]
  2 [ Will there be more than 10 goals?            ]
  [ + Add a question ]
  Each one must have a clear yes or no answer.

Category (required)
  Category: Sports > Soccer   Suggested from your event name.
  [ Search categories, e.g. tennis ]
  [ Crypto · 4 ] [ Culture ] [ Economics ] ... [ Sports · 23 ] ...

Similar markets
  Nothing like this yet.

Creating costs 1.50 USDC (check fee and bond), plus your first
prediction from 1.00 USDC. Your wallet has 12.00 USDC.
```

When you tap **Next**, the quick checks run straight away: lengths, words we don't
allow, a question already listed in the event, the category. Anything they find is
shown on the field before you go on, so nothing is paid for a submission they would
stop.

### Event names

Your event goes live under the name you type, with a little added so every event has a
name of its own:

| Your event | Name it goes live under |
| --- | --- |
| A game with friends (proved by a participant photo or document) | The day and a generated nickname: **Golf with buddies · Sat 28 Nov · Copper Falcon**. Any number of groups can run their own "Golf with buddies" |
| A public event (proved by a news link, an official source or a published document) | The day only: **Federer vs Nadal · Sun 12 Jul** |

The day is left off when your name already has one ("Saturday's parkrun"). If the whole
name is ever taken, a number follows it.

**A public event is listed once.** If a live event already has your event's name —
Calibri's or a member's — creating stops before you pay, and you are sent to **add your
market to it** instead. Names match on their words in any order, ignoring punctuation,
"vs / v / versus" and dates, so "Wimbledon: Nadal v Federer" matches "Federer vs Nadal –
Wimbledon". This applies whatever proof you choose: a public match cannot be filed as a
game with friends. Our review also looks past the wording: an event that is really one
already listed ("Federer vs Nadal with friends", "Nadal–Federer Wimbledon bets") is
stopped after the check, and you are sent to add your market to the listed event. The
bond comes back; the check fee does not.

**Participant proof is only for something you and your friends take part in.** A
parkrun time, a 5-a-side, a golf 4-ball or a pub quiz can be proved by a participant
photo. A professional match, an election or anything about public figures needs proof
anyone can check, and our review stops a submission that uses participant proof for one.

### Templates

A template shows a worked example: an event name, a question, its proof and its
YES rule. It fills nothing in; you write your own event.

| Template | Example | Proof it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **parkrun time** | Will I finish Saturday's parkrun in under 30 minutes? | Official source (the parkrun results page) |
| **5-a-side with friends** | Will the Bibs beat the Skins on Thursday? | Participant photo of the score |
| **Local issue** | Will the Main Rd pothole be fixed by Friday 18:00? | News or web link |
| **Sports match** | Will the Springboks beat the All Blacks on Saturday? | Official source (the match report) |

### Categories

**A category is required**, chosen from Calibri's existing two-level list — for
example **Sports > Tennis** — not typed as free text. The top-level categories sit in
one row; one with sub-categories (Sports has more than 20) opens them below it, and
the search finds either level. A suggestion from your event
name is preselected and you can change it. Local and civic issues such as a pothole
on Main Rd go under the top-level **Local** category.

The category is for browsing only: it never changes what anyone pays. Every
community market is charged the same 8% taker rate, whatever its category. When you
add a market to an existing event, it takes that event's category.

### Similar markets

As you type, the wizard searches for markets that already ask the same question. The
search is free.

| What it finds | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A market **in the same event** — Calibri's or a member's | **Stopped.** You cannot create a duplicate inside an event; you are linked to the existing market. Nothing is paid |
| A Calibri market elsewhere | A suggestion: **Trade this instead**. You can still continue |
| A community market elsewhere | A suggestion: **Join**. You can still continue |
| Nothing | Continue |

### Your team

Your team posts the result with you. Once your event is live you get an **invite
link**, on the "You're live" screen and on the event page. Share it with the friends
you want on your team: anyone signed in who opens it can **join your team** for that
event, up to 10 people besides you. Nobody joins without opening the link. You can
replace the link at any time: the old one stops working, and people who already
joined stay on the team.

When you name people in your questions:

| Who you name | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| A Calibri member | Use a placeholder such as "Player 1", as for a private person |
| A private person who is not a member | No surnames; "Player 1" is preferred |
| A public figure, in their public role | Neutral wording tied to a public record. No allegations, no private life |
| A party to a public case (a defendant, litigant or official in a widely reported case, ruling or inquiry) | Full name allowed, only in neutral wording about the official outcome. Never their private life, location or contact details |
| Not sure whether public or private | Treated as private |

Anyone named may ask to be anonymised: the name is replaced by a placeholder and the
market keeps trading. See [Community terms](/community/terms#named-people).

## Step 2: How will we know the answer?

```
Step 2 of 3 · How will we know the answer?

Proof (shown to everyone before they predict)

  Anyone can find it
  ( ) News or web link      an article or page you name now
  ( ) Official source       one official page, e.g. a results page
  ( ) Published document    a gazette or council notice

  Only the people involved have it
  (o) Participant photo     taken with the Calibri camera when you post
  ( ) Participant document  e.g. club results

When
  Trading closes at    [ Thu 26 Nov 2026  07:00 ]
  Suggested from your question: set it to when the game starts.

Rules
  1 Will the Bibs beat the Skins on Thursday?
    Resolves YES if [ the photo of the final score shows the Bibs ]
                    [ with more goals than the Skins             ]
    Written for you from your question and proof. Check it.
    [ Write it again for me ]

Who posts the answer
  Once your event is live you get an invite link to share.

More options
  Result expected when trading closes · only your team can add
  markets · not Mature · void if the event is cancelled
```

**The close time is suggested from your words.** A question that names a day ("on
Thursday", "by Friday 18:00", "tonight") sets the close for you; a day without a time
closes at 07:00 that day, before most things start. Change it to when the game or
event actually starts.

### Proof types

You declare **one proof type** for the event. Traders see it before they predict, and the
evidence posted with the result **must be of that type**. It binds both sides: it is
the primary proof for your result and for anyone who disputes it. Evidence of another
type counts as supporting material only.

| Proof type | Who can get it | Example | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **News or web link** | Anyone | A local news article on the pothole | You name the allowed sources, or choose "any reputable outlet". The page is archived with a timestamp |
| **Official source** | Anyone | The electoral commission's results page | You name one URL or domain |
| **Published document** | Anyone | A gazette or council notice | Converted to page images, scanned and archived |
| **Participant photo** | Participants only | The golf scorecard | Taken in the app when you post, with the capture time recorded |
| **Participant document** | Participants only | A club results sheet | Converted to page images, scanned and archived |

<Note>
  **Adding to someone else's event?** On a Calibri event, or on an Open event whose team you are not on, your market may use only proof anyone can find: a news or web link, an official source or a published document. Participant proof stays with the people who were there.
</Note>

A **participant photo** is taken with the in-app camera when you post, and its
capture time is recorded. A photo picked from your gallery counts as supporting
evidence only. Markets proved by participants carry a **proved by participant photo**
label, because a trader who was not there cannot check the result themselves.

What evidence is accepted and how it is checked: [Evidence and privacy](/community/evidence-and-privacy).

### The two times

| Time | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Trading closes at** | People can trade until this time, including while the event is being played. It must be **at least 5 minutes and at most 3 days from now**. It cannot be moved, and the market cannot be closed early |
| **Result expected by** | When the answer will be known, under **More options**. It starts as the closing time; set it up to **7 days later** if the answer only comes afterwards |

Your team can post the result **from the close until 24 hours after Result expected
by**. If nobody on the team posts in that window, traders are asked for the answer
instead and you lose your creator reward. See
[If your team does not post](/community/resolution-and-disputes#if-the-creators-team-does-not-post).

<Note>
  **Set the close for the latest likely end.** A market trades until its close whatever happens in the event. On a game whose result the players know first — a participant-proof market — close trading at the expected end of the game, so nobody can trade on a result they already know. Use **Result expected by** as the buffer if the result takes longer.
</Note>

A market added to an existing event must close **no later than the event's close**.

### Rules for each question

Each question gets its own rules: **Resolves YES if…**, **Otherwise it resolves NO**,
and an optional **Void if…** (under **More options**, with a standard wording already
set). Once you pick the proof, we write a first version of each YES rule from your
question and proof. Check it and edit it so a stranger could check the answer from
your proof; **Write it again for me** replaces it with a fresh version.

A void pays **0.50 USDC per contract to both sides**. It is not a refund: someone who
bought YES at 0.80 loses 0.30 per contract on a void.

These rules are the **market rules**, shown on the event page. The standard
[community terms](/community/terms) apply to every community market alongside them;
there is no separate terms page per market.

### Who can add markets

When your event's proof is a participant photo or document, **More options** on
step 2 also asks who may add markets to it (Team only unless you change it):

```
Who can add markets to this event?
  (o) Team only   you and the friends who joined from your invite link
  ( ) Open        anyone, but only with proof anyone can find

  Everyone can see and trade every market either way.
```

The choice is made here, at creation, and **cannot be changed once the event is
live.** An event proved by something anyone can find is always Open. See
[Open and Team only](/community/open-and-team-only).

## Step 3: Review and sign

The review shows the event card as it will appear, what you pay now, what you can
earn and what happens next. **You approve once**, and the checks run after that.

```
You pay now                                    one payment
  Check fee        0.50 USDC
  Bond             1.00 USDC
  Total            1.50 USDC    not refundable once your
                                event is live
Your first prediction                   [ Change ]
  YES at 30c · 3.00 USDC

What you earn
  10% of the trading fees on your markets, paid when your
  answers are final and nobody has overturned them.

[x] I understand this market and my evidence are public and
    permanent; I have not included personal information or
    illegal content.                               (required)
```

- **One payment, one signing prompt.** The check fee and the bond go to Calibri in a single USDC transfer from your Safe, approved at the same prompt as your first prediction. You sign it with your passkey or your own wallet — the owner of your Safe — never with a [session key](/non-custodial/session-keys).
- **It is a payment, not a deposit.** Calibri holds nothing on your behalf. Once your event is live, neither the fee nor the bond comes back, whatever the outcome.
- **The checkbox is required.** Everything on a community market is public and permanent. See [Evidence and privacy](/community/evidence-and-privacy#public-and-permanent).

<Warning>
  **This cannot be undone.** Once your event is live you cannot edit, cancel or close it early. The title, questions, rules, outcomes and close time are fixed. Read the preview before you sign.
</Warning>

### Your first prediction

Your first prediction goes on any one market of the event and gives it a price from the
start. **The minimum is 1 USDC.** It starts as YES at 50c for 1 USDC on your first
question; tap **Change** to pick another question, side, price or amount.

```
Which question?    [ Will Player A win?            v ]
I think            (o) YES    ( ) NO
My price           [ 30c ]    same as odds of 7 to 3 against
Amount             [ 3 ] USDC                 minimum 1 USDC

  If YES: you get 10 USDC back, a profit of 7 USDC.
  If NO:  you lose your 3 USDC.
  Others can take the other side of this prediction.
```

The first prediction is an ordinary order from your Safe. It is **placed when the event goes
live**. Your balance is checked when you sign, and nothing is locked in between: if
the balance no longer covers the prediction at go-live, the market goes live without it and
shows that the first prediction was not placed.

A market you add to an event later needs its own first prediction.

## The content check

Every submission is checked automatically before it goes live, by two AI models from
different vendors. If either blocks, it is blocked. The check covers the title, the
questions and the rules:

1. **Policy** — the [prohibited list](/community/terms#prohibited-content).
2. **Language** — no swear words, slurs or crude language, on any event, Mature ones included, and every word written normally. Disguised words such as "fuuuck", "a$$hole" or "sh1t" are refused at once, before anything is charged. Names, places, brands and abbreviations are fine ("Kalk Bay", "SA20").
3. **Names** — whether each person named is a public figure, a party to a public matter, or a private person. A public matter of record may be asked about in neutral wording: **"Will Lindsay Clancy be convicted?"** passes, because it asks for the official outcome of a widely reported case. **"Will Lindsay Clancy be doxxed?"** is blocked, because it exposes a person. Victims and witnesses are never named, and wording that presumes guilt is blocked.
4. **The harm-path test** — could anyone make YES happen by harming someone or breaking the law? If so, it is blocked. This is what stops a market from working as a bounty.
5. **Defamation** — wording that asserts or presumes wrongdoing by a named person. Asking whether an official body will reach an outcome ("Will X be charged?") is neutral.
6. **Category** — for a new event, whether its category fits. If it doesn't, the block names a better one and you can switch to it in one tap.
7. **Personal information** — ID numbers, phone numbers, email addresses, bank or card numbers and home addresses, including ones disguised as "zero eight two…" or "name at mail dot com". See [Evidence and privacy](/community/evidence-and-privacy#personal-information).
8. **Relevance** — for a market added to an existing event, or created with a new one: is it about that event? See [Relevance](#relevance).

The result is **pass** or **block**. A borderline result is a block; there is no
staff review at creation.

### A block and its rewrite

A block shows the reason and a suggested rewrite you can accept in one tap:

```
Not listed yet

  Question 2
  "Will Sipho finally get caught cheating?"

  Why: it implies wrongdoing by a named person.

  Try this instead
  "Will Player 2 finish with the lowest score?"

  [ Use this wording ]        [ Write my own ]

  Rewrites left: 3     Time left: 14:32     No extra charge
```

You have **15 minutes and up to 3 rewrites**, at no extra charge and with no second
signature.

| Result | What happens | Money |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pass, first time or after a rewrite | The event goes live and your first prediction is placed | The check fee and bond are Calibri's; neither comes back |
| The third rewrite is blocked, or the 15 minutes run out | The submission is closed. No event exists | The **1 USDC bond is paid back** to your Safe with the next daily payout. The 0.50 USDC check fee is kept. No first prediction is placed |

Every rewrite is kept with its check result, the reasons, the suggested rewrite and
the times.

### Relevance

When you add a market to an existing event — Calibri's or another member's — the
wizard asks one required line:

```
How does this market relate to this event?   (required)
  [ It is about a play in this game, decided by the box score ]

  Your market must be about this game and be decided by it,
  or within its time. Off-topic markets are refused.
```

The content check then gives a **relevance verdict**. A market passes when it is
about the event's subject — the same teams, players or occasion — **and** is decided
by the event or within its time.

| Added to "Red Sox vs Yankees" | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| "Will there be a grand slam?" | Passes: a play in this game, decided by the box score |
| "Will South Africa win the cricket on Saturday?" | Refused: not about this game |
| "Will the Red Sox make the playoffs next season?" | Refused: not decided by this game or within its time |

A refusal works like any other block: rewrite it within your 3 rewrites and 15
minutes, covered by the one check fee. Markets created together with a new event are
checked against that event too, without the extra line.

<Note>
  **Very short in-play markets are not possible.** A community market closes only at a scheduled time, so "Will the next pitch be a strike?" cannot be created. Game-level and inning-level markets with an official source are fine.
</Note>

## After the event is live

| Action | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Edit | Not possible. Title, questions, rules, outcomes and close time are fixed |
| Cancel | Not possible, for an event or a market |
| Close early | Not possible. A market closes only at its scheduled time |
| Add a market | Allowed until the event closes, by whoever its [Open or Team only](/community/open-and-team-only) setting allows, within the caps below. Each added market needs its own check fee and first prediction, and must close no later than the event |

Once live you get a share link, a QR code, your team's invite link and a reminder
before your posting deadline. To run a similar event again, use **Create another like
this** on the event's card in your Community hub: it copies the name, questions, rules,
proof and category, and you set the new times and first prediction.

## Limits and caps

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Active events per member | 3 |
| Markets per member per event | 5, under one bond |
| Community markets per community event | 20, your team included |
| Community markets per Calibri event | 50 |
| Submissions | 10 per account per day |
| Rewrites per submission | 3, within 15 minutes |
| First prediction | At least 1 USDC |
| Trading closes | At least 1 hour and at most 3 days from now |
| Result expected by | At most 7 days after the close |

- **An event is active until your last market in it closes.** Markets you add to a Calibri event count as one of your 3 active events, as an event of your own does.
- **A full event** shows **This event is full** and sends you to its existing markets. Nothing is paid.
- **A Mature market must go in a Mature event.** A market added to an event must match the event's Mature flag, or it is refused.
- **Repeated blocks cost more.** Every blocked check counts, rewrites included. After 3 blocks in 24 hours your next check costs 1.10 USDC; after 5, submissions are suspended for 24 hours.
- **A takedown, or two abandoned events within 30 days,** suspends creation for 30 days. See [Community terms](/community/terms#bans-and-suspensions).

Limits on this page are configured by Calibri and may change. Money settings in force
when you create an event — the taker rate, the creator reward percentage, the fees
and bonds — are recorded on it and **never change for that event**.
