These are the standard terms for **every community market**: the same for each one,
whoever created it. They are the community section of Calibri's
[Terms of use](/terms-of-use), and everything there applies to community markets too.
A market's own rules — its question and its "Resolves YES if", "Otherwise NO" and
"Void if" text — decide that market; these terms apply alongside them. There is no
separate terms page per market.

<Note>
  The full legal text is published at [news.calibri.io/terms](https://news.calibri.io/terms/). This article covers the same ground in plain language; where the two differ, the published terms govern.
</Note>

## Who may take part

- **Anyone may trade** a community market. Every community market is publicly listed.
- **To create a market or dispute one you need a self-custodial wallet** — your own [Safe](/non-custodial/the-safe). A member on a custodial balance cannot create or dispute, but may add evidence to a dispute and answer when a creator stays silent.
- **To dispute a result** you must have traded in that market (at least one fill) or hold a position in it.

## Public and permanent

- **A community market and everything posted on it are public**: the title, the market rules, the result, every piece of evidence and every dispute statement.
- **Evidence is stored permanently** on public storage that neither you nor Calibri can delete.
- Calibri hides evidence older than 12 months **on its own pages only**. The copies on public storage and the public case logs remain public.
- **Names and titles are never written to the blockchain.** Only a market id, a hash of the market rules and an ID-only link to the market's event page are.

By creating a market, posting a result or filing a dispute, you confirm that you
understand this, and that you have not included personal information or illegal
content.

## A live market is fixed

**A live market cannot be edited, cancelled or closed early.** Its title, questions,
rules, outcomes and close time are fixed once it is live. Markets may still be added
to a live event until it closes, by whoever the event's
[Open or Team only](/community/open-and-team-only) setting allows.

## Fees, bonds and your funds

- **The check fee and the dispute fee are not refundable.**
- **The creator bond and the dispute bond are non-refundable payments to Calibri**, like the fees. They are not deposits.
- **Calibri never holds your funds.** Nothing you pay is held on your behalf, and there is no escrow. Your USDC stays in your own Safe until you trade or pay.
- **Payouts are rewards.** The creator reward, a winning disputer's payout, a creator bond paid back after a blocked submission and a late dispute payment paid back are paid by Calibri to your Safe, under the rules in [Fees, bonds and rewards](/community/fees-bonds-and-rewards).
- **The creator reward** is paid only when a market ends final and clean, and is forfeited or passed to a disputer in the cases listed there.
- **Money settings are fixed per event** when it is created. A later change applies to new events only.

## Prohibited content

A community market may not be about, or include:

- **Harm to a person** — death, injury, assault, kidnapping; the death or health of public figures.
- **Illegal acts** — a crime as the subject: whether one will be committed, or whether someone will commit, suffer or get away with one (drug deals, theft, vandalism, fraud, bribery, evading police).
- **Outcomes reachable through a crime** — any market where breaking the law could produce the result.
- **Defamation** — wording that asserts or presumes wrongdoing by a named person.
- **Harassment, bullying and exposing anyone** — doxxing, leaking or finding where someone lives, publishing contact details, campaigns against a person. This applies whoever is named.
- **Victims and witnesses** — naming, describing or asking about them, including in a public case.
- **Private life** — relationships, pregnancy, sexuality, health, even with consent.
- **Minors.**
- **Sexual, hate and self-harm content.**
- **Anything Calibri's licence does not permit.**

**Crime as a subject vs a public legal process.** A crime is never the subject of a
market, but the **official outcome** of a public process about one may be: a verdict,
sentence, charge, ruling, appeal, extradition or inquiry finding, asked in neutral
wording and resolved from the public record. This holds even when the case involves
death, injury or children, as long as the market does not name, describe or ask about
the victims or the harm itself.

| Market | |
| --- | --- |
| "Will Lindsay Clancy be convicted?" | Allowed: the outcome of a public case |
| "Will Lindsay Clancy be doxxed?" | Not allowed: exposes a person |
| "Will the killer X get life?" | Not allowed: presumes guilt |
| "Will the children's names be released?" | Not allowed: about the victims |

**The harm-path test.** Every market is asked one question: **could anyone make YES
happen by harming someone or breaking the law?** If so, it may not be listed. This is
what stops a market from working as a bounty.

Every submission is checked against these rules before it goes live, and a
borderline result is treated as a block. See
[The content check](/community/creating-a-market#the-content-check).

## Named people

| Who is named | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| A public figure, in their public role | Neutral wording tied to a public record. No allegations, no private life |
| A Calibri member | Must be tagged. A placeholder such as "Player 1" is shown until they accept |
| A private person who is not a member | No surnames; "Player 1" is preferred |
| Unclear whether public or private | Treated as private |

- **Anyone named may ask to be anonymised.** Their name is replaced by a placeholder at once. The market is not voided or hidden, and trading continues.
- A named person who is not a member can ask through the public form at [calibri.io/report](https://calibri.io/report), verified by email.
- Events naming private people are not indexed by search engines.

## Personal information

You must not include personal information in a market or in evidence: ID numbers,
phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, bank or card numbers, faces or number
plates of people who have not agreed, or information about children. You must not
include anything illegal. Attempts to disguise such details are blocked too.

Uploads are checked before they are stored. Calibri may block an upload, hide
evidence on its pages and take a market down. **All faces and number plates are
blurred**, which may occasionally obscure proof. See
[Evidence and privacy](/community/evidence-and-privacy).

## Results, disputes and finality

- **Who may post a result.** Only the creator's team — the creator and tagged members who accepted — from the close until 24 hours after the result-expected time. On a Team only event, the event's team posts every market; on an Open or Calibri event, each member who added markets posts their own.
- **If nobody on the team posts,** the creator reward is forfeited, holders are asked for the answer, and the AI panel decides.
- **A dispute must be started within 30 minutes of a posted result**, extended once by 30 minutes if the AI watcher flags it, and must carry evidence. Its payment must confirm within 10 minutes of the claim. Any trader in the market may add evidence to it.
- **The evidence of the declared proof type is the primary proof** for both sides.
- **An AI panel's ruling is final.** There is no appeal to UMA or any other arbiter. The on-chain resolver's fallback — escalation to UMA, or a forced void — applies only if Calibri fails to report an outcome by the market's registered deadline.
- **Calibri does not decide community outcomes, except in four listed exceptional cases** — a court order or legal requirement, an illegal-content takedown, proven fraud or exploit, or an AI, vendor or system failure — and shows every such override on the market with its action, reason and date. An override is possible only before the outcome is reported on the blockchain; after that the outcome is final.
- **Disputers are paid under the published rules**: a disputer who wins is paid back everything they paid plus the creator reward; one who neither wins nor loses gets the bond back; one who loses gets nothing.
- **A void pays 0.50 USDC per contract** to both sides. It is not a refund.

The full process: [Resolution and disputes](/community/resolution-and-disputes).

## Reporting, hiding and takedown

- **Every market has a Report button**, with categories matching the [prohibited list](#prohibited-content).
- **Hidden at once** on a harm-category report — violence or bounty-type markets, illegal acts, minors, sexual content — from an account that has deposited and is at least 7 days old.
- **Queued for review without hiding** after 3 reports from distinct qualifying accounts.
- **A hidden market** is removed from browse and search, its page shows "Under review", and new orders are paused. Positions are untouched.
- **Staff decide within 24 hours.** If the report is cleared, the market is relisted and its close time and any open window are extended by the time it was hidden, as far as the market's registered deadline allows.
- **Takedown.** If the report is upheld, the market is unlisted and **voids at 0.50 per contract**. The creator reward is forfeited. If the only problem is a name, the name is anonymised instead.
- **False reports.** A cleared report counts against the reporter. After 3 cleared reports in 30 days, that account's reports no longer hide anything.

## Bans and suspensions

| What happens | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| A market is taken down | No creating for 30 days; **permanently** on a repeat |
| Two abandoned events within 30 days | No creating for 30 days |
| Evidence marked fabricated by the AI panel, from the creator or an account linked to them | Creator fraud: the ruling goes against the creator, the creator reward goes to the disputer, and a **permanent** creation ban |
| Creator fraud proven outside a dispute, acted on by an admin override | The creator reward is forfeited, and a **permanent** creation ban |
| Evidence marked fabricated by the AI panel | The submitting account loses dispute and evidence rights **permanently** |
| 3 lost disputes in 30 days | No disputing for 30 days. An "unsure" ruling does not count |
| 5 blocked submissions in 24 hours | No submitting for 24 hours |
| 3 blocked uploads in 24 hours | No uploading for 24 hours |
| A match against known child-abuse material | The account is frozen and reported as the law requires |

A ban on creating or disputing does not stop you trading. The fair-use section of the
[Terms of use](/terms-of-use), including the bar on using multiple accounts
to evade limits or gain rewards, applies to community markets in full.
