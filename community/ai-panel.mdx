**A dispute on a community market is decided by three AI models from different
vendors, ruling independently on the same sealed inputs. A 2-of-3 majority, each at
least 0.70 confident, decides, and the ruling is final.** The prompts and the pinned
model versions are published on this page, and every case's full log is public.

The models are not the authority — **the declared proof is.** The panel's job is to
read the proof the market named before anyone traded, and say what it shows.

## Where AI is used

| Task | Models | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Content check**, at creation | 2 | If either blocks, it is blocked. A model error is also a block |
| **Result watcher**, on every posted result | 2 | If either flags, the result is flagged to every holder and the dispute window is extended once |
| **Dispute panel** | 3 | A 2-of-3 majority at 0.70 confidence; one re-run if unsure |
| **Silent-creator answers** | 3 | The same panel decides from the holders' answers |
| **Text extraction** from images and documents | 1 | The text, which then gets the same checks as typed text |
| **Injection screen** on extracted text | 1 | Marks text that addresses instructions to a model |

The vendors are **Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI and Google**. Each task uses the number of
them shown, from different vendors, with one pinned model per vendor.

## What the panel sees

The inputs are **frozen and hashed before the first model is called**, so evidence
added later, a retry or a restart can never change what was ruled on.

- The market's title, rules and declared proof type.
- **Answer A** and **answer B**, each with its statement, its rebuttal and its evidence. The words "creator" and "disputer" do not appear.
- For each evidence item: the stored image, the text extracted from it, whether it was an in-app capture and its recorded capture time, or its archive time for a link — and a label saying who sent it, which side of the market they hold, how old the account is, and any link to either party.
- For proof anyone can find, the declared source itself.

**The panel is instructed to weigh evidence by verifiability, never by volume**, and
to rule on the declared proof type first: other material is supporting only.

## What each model answers

Every model returns the same structured answer:

| Field | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `answer` | `A`, `B` or `cannot_tell` |
| `proof_status` | `adequate`, `missing` or `ambiguous` — about the declared proof |
| `confidence` | 0 to 1 |
| Evidence relied on | The items the answer rests on |

A model may also mark an evidence item as **fabricated**; see
[Fabricated evidence](/community/evidence-and-privacy#fabricated-evidence). A call that
fails is recorded as an error, never counted as `cannot_tell`.

## How the decision is made

Checked in this order:

| Condition | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Two or three models find the market's own "Void if…" rule met | **Void** under that rule. The disputer gets the bond back; the creator reward is paid |
| Two or three models report the declared proof missing or ambiguous | **Void for weak proof.** The disputer wins |
| Two or three agree on A or B, each at or above 0.70 | **That answer.** Final |
| Otherwise, on the first run | **Run once more**, on the same sealed inputs |
| Otherwise, after the re-run | **The posted result stands.** The disputer gets the bond back; it does not count as a loss |
| Two or more calls failed after retries | No decision. The case waits and staff are alerted; a lasting failure is one of the [exceptional cases](/community/resolution-and-disputes#admin-override-exceptional-cases-only) for an admin override |

Counter-evidence of another proof type cannot produce a void by itself. Every outcome
and what it pays: [Resolution and disputes](/community/resolution-and-disputes#every-outcome).

## Hidden instructions in evidence

Members' statements and the text read out of their evidence are passed to the models
as **marked, untrusted data**, never as instructions. Extracted text is screened for
instructions addressed to a model before the case runs, statements have length
limits, and every answer must fit the fixed structure above. These measures reduce
the risk of a planted instruction steering a ruling; they do not remove it entirely.

## The case log

Each case produces **one public log, stored permanently**: the sealed inputs, the
A/B label map, the prompt and model versions, every request and raw response for
each run, the decision and the rule applied. It is linked from the market's
resolution panel on calibri.io. The parties' statements are in it, and are therefore
public and permanent.

**Re-running a case.** Anyone can resend a case's requests to the same model versions.
They may not get the same answer — models do not always answer identically, and
vendors retire models — so the stored responses, not a re-run, are the record.

## Versions in use

The panel runs **pinned** model versions: a vendor's newer model is not used until
the configuration names it. Each case log records the exact versions that ruled on it.

## What leaves Calibri

Every disputed case sends the members' statements and evidence to three AI vendors,
and every posted result sends its evidence to two. Uploads also pass through
moderation and hash-matching services before they are stored. See
[Evidence and privacy](/community/evidence-and-privacy#who-sees-what).
