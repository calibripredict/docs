A **position** is any holding of outcome tokens in your wallet. Every time you buy or sell a Yes or No token on Calibri, your position in that market changes. Understanding how positions work — how to measure their value, adjust their size, and ultimately close or redeem them — is essential to managing your prediction market portfolio with confidence.

## What Is a Position?

A position represents the quantity of a specific outcome token you hold in a given market. Calibri tracks positions at the token level: your Yes position and your No position in a market are recorded separately, each identified by its ERC-1155 `tokenId`.

Here is an example position object:

```json
{
  "conditionId": "0xdeadbeef00000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000001",
  "tokenId": "52114319501245915516055106046884209969926127482827954674443846427813813222426",
  "outcomeIndex": 0,
  "size": "250.00",
  "avgPrice": "0.58",
  "currentPrice": "0.65",
  "pnl": "17.50"
}
```

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `conditionId` | Onchain condition ID of the market |
| `tokenId` | ERC-1155 token ID — identifies this as a Yes (index 0) or No (index 1) token |
| `outcomeIndex` | `0` = Yes, `1` = No |
| `size` | Number of tokens you hold |
| `avgPrice` | Your average entry price in pUSD per token |
| `currentPrice` | Current mid-price of the token in pUSD |
| `pnl` | Unrealized profit or loss in pUSD |

## P&L Calculation

Calibri computes **unrealized P&L** on any open position as:

```text
unrealized P&L = (currentPrice - avgPrice) × size
```

Using the example above: `(0.65 - 0.58) × 250 = $17.50` unrealized profit.

Your **average price** updates with every fill. If you buy 100 tokens at $0.55 and then another 150 at $0.60, your average price becomes:

```text
avgPrice = (100 × 0.55 + 150 × 0.60) / 250 = 0.58
```

After the market resolves, P&L becomes **realized**:

- **Winning position**: `realized P&L = (1.00 - avgPrice) × size`
- **Losing position**: `realized P&L = (0.00 - avgPrice) × size` (your full cost basis is lost)

For example, 250 Yes tokens bought at an average of $0.58 that resolve as winners: `(1.00 - 0.58) × 250 = $105.00` realized profit.

## Split, Merge, and Redeem

Calibri gives you three ways to move between pUSD and outcome tokens without going through the order book.

### Split

<Steps>
  <Step title="Approve pUSD">
    Approve the Calibri contract to spend $1.00 pUSD from your wallet for each pair of tokens you want to create.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call split">
    Submit the split transaction. The contract locks your pUSD and mints an equal quantity of Yes and No tokens to your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive tokens">
    You now hold 1 Yes token + 1 No token per $1.00 pUSD deposited. You can sell one or both sides on the order book.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Splitting is most useful for **market makers**: split pUSD, post the Yes tokens as asks and the No tokens as asks on the opposite side, and earn the spread when both sides fill.

### Merge

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hold equal quantities">
    Ensure you hold at least N Yes tokens and N No tokens for the same market.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call merge">
    Submit the merge transaction specifying the quantity N. The contract burns N Yes + N No tokens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive pUSD">
    You receive N pUSD back in your wallet — no trading fees, no spread cost.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use merge to exit a balanced position (equal Yes and No holdings) without crossing the spread. Instead of selling both sides on the book and paying taker fees twice, merging returns $1.00 pUSD per pair at zero cost.
</Tip>

### Redeem

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait for resolution">
    Hold your winning outcome tokens until the market resolves and payouts are enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call redeem">
    Submit the redeem transaction for your winning token. The contract burns your tokens and transfers $1.00 pUSD per token to your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm receipt">
    Your pUSD balance increases by `size × $1.00`. Losing tokens are worthless and can be ignored (or burned to clean up your wallet).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Combo Positions

A **combo position** is a multi-leg position that combines outcome tokens from two or more markets to express a more nuanced view. For example:

- **Correlated long**: buy Yes in Market A and Yes in Market B if you believe both outcomes are likely.
- **Spread trade**: buy Yes in Market A and No in Market A at the same time as part of a market-making strategy.
- **Negative risk hedge**: in a mutually exclusive event, hold No tokens across multiple markets to effectively hold a diversified Yes basket.

Calibri does not currently offer a native combo order type — you build combo positions by placing individual orders across markets. When tracking P&L for a combo, sum the `pnl` fields across each constituent position object.

## Holding Rewards

Calibri periodically runs **holding reward** programs that pay eligible token holders for maintaining positions in designated markets. Rewards are distributed based on a snapshot of your token balances at a specified block height, and credited to your account automatically.

To check whether a market is part of an active holding reward program, inspect the `rewardsProgram` field on the market object. When eligible, your portfolio dashboard will display accrued rewards alongside your standard P&L.
