---
title: "Creator score"
description: "The public score every community-market creator carries — one number built from how cleanly their events resolve, shown as New until they have three resolved events, and lowered by overturns, weak proof, abandonments and takedowns."
---

**Every member who creates community markets has a public creator score**: one
headline number, from 0 to 100%, built from their record of resolving events
cleanly. It sits next to their username on every community market they create, so
you can judge a creator before you trade their market.

## What is scored: events, not markets

The score counts **resolved events**. A creator's event is their group of markets in
one event: a whole community event they created, or the markets they added to a
Calibri event. Five markets in one event are one event for the score.

An event is **resolved** once every one of the creator's markets in it is final. It
then counts as exactly one of these, checked in this order:

| The event counts as | When | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Takedown** | Any of its markets was taken down for breaking the [content rules](/community/terms) | Strongly negative |
| **Overturned** | Otherwise, any market was overturned on dispute, voided for weak proof, or ruled against the creator for fraud | Negative |
| **Abandoned** | Otherwise, any market whose result nobody on the team posted — holders answered, or it voided with no answers | Negative |
| **Clean** | Otherwise: every posted result stood | Positive |

A market voided by its own **"Void if…"** rule, or cancelled, says nothing about the
creator and is left out. An event whose markets were all left out is not counted.

## How the score is calculated

```
score = (clean − 2 × (overturned + abandoned) − 5 × takedowns) ÷ resolved
```

where each term counts events, and `resolved` is the total of the four. The result
is shown as a percentage and **never goes below 0%**.

### Example

A creator with 12 resolved events: 11 clean and 1 overturned.

```
(11 − 2 × 1) ÷ 12 = 9 ÷ 12 = 75%
```

An overturned or abandoned event weighs twice, and a takedown five times, against
each clean one. A dispute that fails does not lower the score: the result stood.

## "New"

Until a creator has **3 resolved events**, their markets show **New** in place of a
number.

```
| by golfer_dave   Score 75%  12 resolved      |
| by new_member    New                         |
```

## Only the creator is scored

The score belongs to the member who created the markets. **Tagged team members carry
no mark**, whoever on the team posted the result. On a Team only event, a market a
team member added counts toward that member's own score, as its creator.

## Where it shows

| Where | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Event cards | The creator's username, score and number of resolved events |
| Event pages | The same, on each community market — including in the Community markets section of a Calibri event |
| The creator's profile | The score and the full record: events created, clean, disputed, overturned, abandoned, takedowns, total traded volume and account age |
| The Community tab | A sort option by creator score |

The name shown is the member's **username**.

## What it does not do

- **It does not raise any limit.** Every creator has the same limits — 3 active events, 5 markets per event — whatever their score. There are no tiers.
- **It does not change what anyone pays.** The fees, bonds and the creator reward are the same at every score.

The score is a record, not a gate. A market's traders are protected by the
[dispute process](/community/resolution-and-disputes) and the AI watcher at every
score.
