title: "Rewards"
description: "Three ways to earn on Calibri — maker rebates and referral rewards, both a share of the taker fee, and liquidity rewards on markets with a liquidity pool. All are paid by a daily sweep."
---

Calibri pays three kinds of reward. Two are funded out of the **taker fee** on a
trade; they **accrue live** as you trade and are **paid out once a day** —
independent of whether the market has resolved.

- **Maker rebate** — When **your resting order gets filled**, you earn a share of the taker's fee — **15%, 20% or 25%** depending on the market's category. This is the reward for **providing liquidity** — quoting a price and waiting for someone to trade with you. Full mechanics, rates and worked examples: [Maker rebates](/concepts/maker-rebates).
- **Referral reward** — When someone **you referred trades as the taker**, **you (the referrer)** earn a share of the fee they paid. Tied to your referred members' taker activity.
- **Liquidity reward** — On markets that have a liquidity pool, resting buy orders within the market's spread band earn a share of a fixed pool for that market. Your score in a market is the larger of your smaller side and a third of your larger side, so quoting both sides evenly earns full credit and quoting one side earns a third; while the market's mid is below 0.15 or above 0.85, no order earns. While you have a resting sell order on any market, none of your orders earn a liquidity reward. It is paid daily, like the other rewards. It is not funded from the taker fee.

## Maker and referral rewards are a share of the taker fee

| Reward | Who earns it | Based on |
|---|---|---|
| **Maker rebate** | the **maker** — the resting (non-taker) side of the fill | a % of the taker fee on that fill |
| **Referral reward** | the **taker's referrer** | a % of the taker fee on the referred taker's fill |

Because both are a percentage **of the taker fee**, and the fee follows the
`P × (1 − P)` curve, both rewards **inherit that curve**: largest on coin-flip
prices, near zero at the extremes, and zero on a trade that pays no fee at all
(a fee-free category — see [Fees](/concepts/fees)).

<Note>
  Rates are set **per market category**. The maker rebate is **20%** in Crypto, **15%** in Sports and **25%** elsewhere — see [Maker rebates](/concepts/maker-rebates) for the table. Referral rates are published in the rewards schedule and shown in-app.
</Note>

## How and when you get paid

The reward flow is deliberately **decoupled from settlement** — you don't have to
wait for a market to resolve to get paid.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accrue at the trade">
    When a fill pays a taker fee, the maker rebate and referral reward are
    recorded as **pending** entries. No money moves for the reward yet — only the taker fee moves at trade
    time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it tick up live">
    Pending rewards are readable in near-real-time via the **rewards / pending**
    endpoint, so a market maker can watch their pending balance grow as they
    quote. The response separates the three sources:
  </Step>
</Steps>

```json
{ "currency": "usdc", "total": "13.10", "maker": "10.00", "referral": "2.35", "liquidity": "0.75" }
```

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get paid daily">
    Pending rewards are **paid out daily** — whether or not the market has
    resolved. Liquidity rewards arrive a day later than maker rebates and
    referral rewards. This deliberately rewards early and continuous quoting
    rather than waiting for markets to settle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paid to your Safe">
    Rewards are paid **on-chain to your Safe**. A member with no registered Safe
    yet stays accrued until one exists — nothing is lost.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Minimums

There is **no payout floor**. The only rounding is at accrual: a reward smaller
than one base unit (`0.000001 USDC`) rounds away.

## What happens if a market is voided

If a market is **voided**, every share pays 0.50 and the taker fee on a settled trade
is kept (see [Voids and postponements](/non-custodial/resolution#voids-and-postponements)).
Rewards on settled trades are unaffected: the fee that funds them is kept, so pending
rewards are paid as normal and paid rewards stay yours. A trade not yet settled
on-chain is unwound instead: its fee is refunded and its pending rewards are
cancelled.

## In short

> **Make markets, earn a rebate.** When your resting order gets filled, you earn
> a share of the taker's fee. **Refer traders, earn a share** of the fees on
> their trades. Rewards **accrue live** and are **paid out once a day**, whether
> or not the market has settled, landing in your Safe. Void a market and you get 0.50 per share and keep any rewards already paid; the taker fee on a settled trade is not returned.
