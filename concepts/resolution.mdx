When a Calibri market closes, the outcome must be determined and recorded onchain before winning tokens can be redeemed. Calibri uses the **UMA Optimistic Oracle** — a decentralized, permissionless system — to perform this resolution. Rather than relying on a trusted admin to declare the winner, UMA uses a cryptoeconomic challenge-and-dispute mechanism that lets anyone propose an outcome and anyone else challenge it if they believe it is wrong.

## Resolution Oracle

The **UMA Optimistic Oracle** works on an "optimistic" principle: a proposed answer is assumed correct unless someone puts up a bond to dispute it within a defined window. This design is both gas-efficient and robust — most markets resolve quickly because the outcome is obvious, while contentious markets escalate through a structured debate process.

Key properties of the UMA Optimistic Oracle:

- **Permissionless.** Any address can propose an outcome and any address can dispute it.
- **Bond-secured.** Both proposers and disputers must post a pUSD bond, creating a financial incentive to submit only correct answers.
- **Community-governed.** Escalated disputes are settled by a vote of UMA token holders, the protocol's decentralized governance layer.
- **Onchain final.** Once confirmed, the resolution is recorded onchain and cannot be reversed.

## Resolution Timeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Market closes">
    The market's `endDate` is reached, or an admin manually closes the market. Trading stops. Existing positions are frozen until the outcome is confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Proposer submits outcome">
    Any participant submits a proposed outcome (Yes or No) along with a **$750 pUSD bond**. The bond is slashed if the proposal is successfully disputed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2-hour challenge window">
    A challenge period begins. Anyone who believes the proposed outcome is incorrect can dispute it by posting a matching bond. If no dispute arrives within 2 hours, the outcome is automatically confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Outcome confirmed (undisputed)">
    No dispute was filed. The outcome is finalized onchain and payouts are immediately enabled. Winning token holders can now redeem $1.00 pUSD per token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="First dispute — debate period (if disputed)">
    A disputer has challenged the proposal. A second proposal round opens, and both sides have **24–48 hours** to submit supporting evidence and arguments. A new bond is required to advance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Second dispute — UMA token holder vote (if escalated)">
    If the debate does not resolve the dispute, the question is escalated to a full **UMA token holder vote**, which typically takes **~48 hours**. The majority vote determines the final outcome.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Expected resolution time:** ~2 hours for undisputed outcomes; 4–6 days for fully disputed outcomes that reach a token holder vote. Plan your liquidity accordingly if you are holding positions near a market's close date.
</Note>

## Claiming Your Winnings

Once the market is resolved, you redeem winning tokens for $1.00 pUSD each by calling the Calibri redemption contract. Here is an example using the Calibri SDK:

```typescript
import { CalibriClient } from "@calibri/client";

const client = new CalibriClient({ apiKey: process.env.CALIBRI_API_KEY });

// Redeem winning Yes tokens for a resolved market
const receipt = await client.redeem({
  conditionId: "0xdeadbeef00000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000000001",
  tokenId: "52114319501245915516055106046884209969926127482827954674443846427813813222426",
  amount: "250000000", // 250 tokens in base units (6 decimals)
});

console.log(`Redeemed. Transaction hash: ${receipt.transactionHash}`);
console.log(`pUSD received: ${receipt.payoutAmount}`);
```

You can also trigger redemption from the Calibri web interface by navigating to the resolved market and clicking **Redeem Winnings**.

Losing tokens have a redemption value of $0.00. You do not need to take any action for losing tokens — they simply become inert and can be burned to clean up your wallet.

## Edge Cases

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What if the outcome is ambiguous?">
    If the real-world result is genuinely unclear — for example, a market asks "Will the Fed cut rates in October?" and the meeting is postponed indefinitely — UMA may resolve the market at **50/50**. In a 50/50 resolution, both Yes and No tokens redeem for **$0.50 each** rather than the usual $1.00 / $0.00 split. This distributes collateral equally and ensures no party is unfairly disadvantaged by an indeterminate outcome.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I hold both Yes and No tokens?">
    If you hold equal quantities of Yes and No tokens at resolution, you have two options:
    
    1. **Merge before resolution.** Return your Yes + No pair to the contract for $1.00 pUSD — this is the cleanest exit and works any time before the market closes.
    2. **Redeem both after resolution.** If the market resolved normally (one winner), your winning tokens pay $1.00 each and your losing tokens pay $0.00 — net $1.00 pUSD per pair, same as merging. If the market resolved at 50/50, both tokens pay $0.50 — again $1.00 pUSD per pair. Either way, holding both sides is always made whole at $1.00.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if the market never resolves?">
    In rare cases — such as a significant technical failure or a market question that becomes permanently unanswerable — a market may not receive a resolution. In this scenario, the collateral is not lost. Because every token pair is backed 1:1 by locked pUSD, you can always **merge** your Yes and No tokens back into $1.00 pUSD at any time while the contracts are live, regardless of whether the market has resolved.
    
    If you hold only one side, you would need to first acquire the opposite token on the secondary market (or wait for any formal remediation process announced by the Calibri team).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Payout Mechanics

The table below summarizes the redemption value of each token type under every possible resolution outcome.

| Resolution outcome | Yes token value | No token value |
|---|---|---|
| **Yes wins** | $1.00 pUSD | $0.00 |
| **No wins** | $0.00 | $1.00 pUSD |
| **50/50 (ambiguous)** | $0.50 pUSD | $0.50 pUSD |
| **Unresolved (pre-resolution)** | Market price | Market price |

All payouts are denominated in **pUSD** and transferred directly to your wallet upon redemption. There are no platform fees on redemption — you receive the full face value of your winning tokens.
