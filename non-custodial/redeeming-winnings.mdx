---
title: "Redeeming winnings"
description: "Self-custody winners are not auto-paid — sign a redeemPositions call, relay it gaslessly, and withdraw USDC from your Safe."
---

Winnings are **claimed, not delivered**. Because your funds and outcome tokens
live in your own Safe, resolving a market only makes your winning token
**redeemable** — it does not push USDC to you.

You call `redeemPositions` yourself (relayed, gasless), the USDC lands in your
Safe, and then you withdraw.

## Full 1.00 per share — no settlement fee

<Warning>
  A winning share redeems for the **full 1.00 USDC** — there is **no fee at settlement or redemption**. The platform (taker) fee was already charged **once, at trade time**. Disregard any older wording that says winners redeem "1.00 minus a settlement fee".
</Warning>

## Redemption, step by step

The operator does not pull funds into your Safe for you — the redemption is
authorised by you and relayed on your behalf.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Market resolves on-chain">
    The operator reports the outcome. Your winning outcome token becomes
    redeemable for 1.00 USDC/share; the losing token is worth 0.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the redeemPositions call">
    Encode
    `redeemPositions(address collateralToken, bytes32 parentCollectionId, bytes32 conditionId, uint256[] indexSets)`
    with:
    - `collateralToken` = `usdc_address`
    - `parentCollectionId` = `0x0000…0000` (32 zero bytes)
    - `conditionId` = `condition_id` (from the market's ctf-config)
    - `indexSets` = `[1, 2]` — sweeps **both** outcome positions in one
    transaction (the winning side pays out, the losing side is 0)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign it as a Safe SafeTx">
    Wrap the calldata in a Safe `SafeTx` EIP-712 (the same structure as setup
    approvals) with `to` = `conditional_tokens_address`, and sign with your EOA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Relay it (gasless)">
    POST it to the relay endpoint. The operator executes
    `Safe.execTransaction` and pays gas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="USDC lands in your Safe">
    The redeemed USDC is transferred **into your Safe** and mirrored to your
    off-chain [`balance_safe`](/non-custodial/balances).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw">
    Move the USDC from your Safe to an external wallet (see below).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Relay the redemption

**`POST /api/v2/atlas/account/wallet/relay`**

Submits a signed Safe transaction; the operator executes it and pays gas.

```json
{
  "safe": "0x… (your Safe)",
  "to": "0x… (ConditionalTokens address)",
  "value": "0",
  "data": "0x… (encoded redeemPositions call)",
  "operation": 0,
  "signature": "0x… (your EOA SafeTx signature)"
}
```

Atlas rejects with `400 safe does not belong to this member` unless `safe` is
your own derived Safe. Response:

```json
{ "tx": "0x… (submitted tx hash, or null)" }
```

## Withdrawing from the Safe

Because funds live in your own Safe, a withdrawal is a **member-controlled
on-chain USDC transfer out of the Safe** — a Safe `SafeTx` executing
`USDC.transfer(to, amount)`, relayed gaslessly through the same
`ctf/relay` endpoint used for redemption.

When funds leave the Safe on-chain, Calibri's watcher reconciles the off-chain
mirror: it debits `balance_safe` and cancels any resting orders those funds
collateralised.

<Note>
  Withdrawals are done through the generic `ctf/relay` USDC-transfer path described above. Confirm in the Wallet UI whether a dedicated one-click "withdraw from Safe" flow is available for your deployment.
</Note>

## Void

If a contract is voided, the resolution unwinds the position on-chain — a 50/50
unwind of the minted set — returning each side its own collateral.
