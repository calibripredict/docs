Calibri uses **pUSD** as its single trading collateral — a Polygon-native ERC-20 token backed 1:1 by USDC.e. Every trade, split, merge, and redemption is settled on-chain on Polygon, while Calibri's relayer handles all gas fees so you never need to hold MATIC. This guide covers how money moves into and out of your Calibri account, and how the ERC-1155 outcome token lifecycle works from deposit to final redemption.

<Note>
  All settlement operations — including deposits, withdrawals, splits, merges, and redeems — occur on **Polygon mainnet (chain ID 137)**. Make sure your wallet is connected to Polygon before interacting with any Calibri smart contracts.
</Note>

---

## Collateral Overview

**pUSD** is the collateral token that powers every prediction market on Calibri:

- **Standard**: ERC-20 on Polygon
- **Backing**: 1:1 with USDC.e (bridged USDC on Polygon)
- **Precision**: 6 decimal places (same as USDC)
- **Use**: Every order, split, and redemption is denominated in pUSD

When you split $1 pUSD, you receive 1 Yes token and 1 No token. When a market resolves, winning tokens redeem for exactly $1.00 pUSD each. Losing tokens are worth $0.00.

## Depositing Funds

To fund your Calibri account, you bridge supported assets to pUSD on Polygon.

### 1. Check Supported Assets

Retrieve the list of accepted deposit chains and tokens:

```bash
GET https://gamma-api.calibri.com/supported-assets
```

```json
[
  {
    "asset": "USDC",
    "chain": "Ethereum",
    "chainId": 1,
    "contractAddress": "0xA0b86991c6218b36c1d19D4a2e9Eb0cE3606eB48",
    "minDeposit": "10.00"
  },
  {
    "asset": "USDC.e",
    "chain": "Polygon",
    "chainId": 137,
    "contractAddress": "0x2791Bca1f2de4661ED88A30C99A7a9449Aa84174",
    "minDeposit": "1.00"
  }
]
```

### 2. Get a Deposit Address

Generate a deposit address for your account:

```bash
POST https://gamma-api.calibri.com/bridge-addresses
```

```json
{
  "walletAddress": "0xYourWalletAddress",
  "sourceChainId": 1,
  "sourceAsset": "USDC"
}
```

**Response:**

```json
{
  "depositAddress": "0xDeposit123...",
  "expiresAt": "2024-04-02T12:00:00Z",
  "memo": "Send USDC on Ethereum to this address"
}
```

### 3. Check Conversion Rates

Before depositing, check the current conversion rate from your source asset to pUSD:

```bash
GET https://gamma-api.calibri.com/quote?sourceAsset=USDC&sourceChainId=1&amount=1000
```

```json
{
  "sourceAsset": "USDC",
  "sourceAmount": "1000.00",
  "destinationAsset": "pUSD",
  "destinationAmount": "999.50",
  "fee": "0.50",
  "estimatedTimeSeconds": 120
}
```

Send your funds to the deposit address. Calibri's bridge monitors the transaction and credits pUSD to your account after on-chain confirmation.

## Checking Your Balance

Retrieve your current pUSD balance and any locked collateral:

```bash
GET https://clob.calibri.com/balances
```

**Response:**

```json
{
  "walletAddress": "0xYourWalletAddress",
  "availableBalance": "500.00",
  "lockedInOrders": "65.00",
  "lockedInPositions": "250.00",
  "totalBalance": "815.00",
  "currency": "pUSD"
}
```

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `availableBalance` | Free pUSD available for new orders or withdrawals |
| `lockedInOrders` | Collateral reserved by open resting orders |
| `lockedInPositions` | Collateral tied up in outcome token positions |
| `totalBalance` | Sum of all three |

## Withdrawing Funds

To withdraw pUSD from your Calibri account, submit a signed withdrawal request directly to the withdraw endpoint. You specify the destination chain and asset in the request body — no pre-registration step is required.

```bash
POST https://gamma-api.calibri.com/withdraw
```

```json
{
  "walletAddress": "0xYourWalletAddress",
  "amount": "200.00",
  "destinationAddress": "0xYourExternalAddress",
  "destinationChainId": 1,
  "destinationAsset": "USDC",
  "signature": "0xSignedRequest..."
}
```

The `signature` field is an EIP-712 signed authorisation over the withdrawal parameters. The Calibri SDK's `signWithdrawal` helper builds and signs this automatically.

<Warning>
  Bridge withdrawals are processed on-chain and may take **several minutes to complete** depending on network congestion and the destination chain's bridge confirmation requirements. Ethereum mainnet withdrawals may take up to 15 minutes. Do not assume funds are available at the destination immediately after submitting the request.
</Warning>

## Gasless Transactions

Calibri's **relayer** handles all gas fees on your behalf. You sign the transaction payload with your wallet, then submit it to the relayer — the relayer broadcasts it on-chain and pays the MATIC gas cost.

Submit gasless transactions via:

```bash
POST https://relayer-v2.calibri.com/submit
```

The relayer supports the following operation types:

| Operation | Description |
|---|---|
| `wallet_deploy` | Deploy your proxy wallet contract (one-time, on first use) |
| `approval` | Approve the CTF contract to spend your pUSD |
| `split` | Split pUSD into Yes + No outcome tokens |
| `merge` | Merge Yes + No outcome tokens back into pUSD |
| `redeem` | Redeem winning tokens for pUSD after market resolution |

Each relayer response includes the on-chain `txHash` that you can verify independently on [Polygonscan](https://polygonscan.com). Operations typically confirm within a few seconds on Polygon. You can also track your full trade and position history through the CLOB data endpoints — see the [Manage Positions guide](/guides/manage-positions) for details.

## ERC-1155 Outcome Tokens

Calibri's outcome tokens follow the **ERC-1155 multi-token standard** managed by the Conditional Token Framework (CTF) contract on Polygon.

Key technical details:

- Each prediction market generates **two outcome tokens** identified by their `tokenId` — one for Yes (index 0) and one for No (index 1).
- Token IDs are deterministically derived from the market's `conditionId` and the outcome index, so the same market always produces the same token IDs.
- Balances are held in the CTF contract. Your Calibri account balance reflects what the CTF contract records for your wallet address.
- All orders on Calibri's CLOB are **EIP-712 signed messages** — you sign the order struct off-chain, and the CLOB validates the signature before settling the trade on-chain through the CTF contract.

**Split, merge, and redeem operations** interact directly with the CTF contract. Because these are on-chain transactions, they require a valid wallet deployment on Polygon. Calibri's relayer deploys your proxy wallet automatically on your first gasless transaction — you pay no MATIC.

After a market resolves, the CTF contract records the winning outcome. You call `redeem` to instruct the contract to burn your winning tokens and transfer $1.00 pUSD per token to your wallet. Losing tokens cannot be redeemed and have zero value after resolution.
