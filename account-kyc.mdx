Calibri handles identity — sign-up, sessions, and **KYC (Know Your Customer)
verification** — through its identity service. Whether KYC is required, and what
it gates, depends on your jurisdiction and the operator's configuration.

## Why KYC exists

KYC verification confirms who you are. It helps meet financial-services
regulations, prevents fraud and money laundering, and is a standard requirement
for depositing, withdrawing, and trading in regulated markets.

## What KYC can gate

Depending on how the platform is configured for your region, verification may be
required before you can:

- **Place orders** — trading can be gated behind a minimum verification level. If
  it is and you haven't met it, order placement is rejected (the API returns a
  `kyc.required` error).
- **Withdraw funds** — moving money out typically requires verification.

If no KYC threshold is configured for your region, you may be able to trade
without it. **Check your account settings to see the current requirement for your
account** — it is the source of truth, not this page.

<Note>
  Simply browsing markets, reading the order book, and viewing prices never requires KYC — those are open. Verification comes into play when you start moving funds or placing orders.
</Note>

## Completing verification

Verification is done from your account settings. You'll generally be asked to:

1. Provide your identifying details (such as legal name, date of birth, and
   country of residence).
2. Upload a clear photo of a valid, government-issued photo ID.
3. Complete any additional checks the flow requests.

Tips for a smooth first submission:

- Use a **valid, unexpired** government-issued ID.
- Make sure the whole document is in frame, in focus, and well lit — no glare,
  no cropped corners.
- Enter your details **exactly** as they appear on the ID.

Your submission then moves through a review, after which your account shows a
verified (or, if something needs fixing, a rejected) status with a reason.

## After verification

Once verified, the gates above are lifted for your account, subject to any limits
that apply. You can then fund your account and trade — see
[Funding your account](/wallet-deposits) and [Placing orders](/trading-placing-orders).

## Privacy

Your identity documents are collected to verify you and to meet regulatory
obligations. What is collected, who sees it, and how long it is kept is set out
in the [Privacy policy](/privacy-policy).
