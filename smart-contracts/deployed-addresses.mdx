Every contract Calibri trades against is public. You do not have to take custody
claims on trust: read the code, check the balances, and confirm your own Safe
holds what you think it holds.

This page lists what is deployed and shows you how to verify each address for
yourself.

<Note>
  The **API is the authoritative source** for every address on every network. The values below are for reference and verification — if this page and the API ever disagree, the API is right and this page is stale.
</Note>

## Polygon (mainnet)

Calibri settles on **Polygon**, chain id **137**, collateralised in **native
USDC**.

| Contract | Address | Verify |
|---|---|---|
| **CTF Exchange** (Calibri) | `0xB6Ac44d5A6388b8C7511F2fb20C0c03fD0CeDC83` | [View on Polygonscan](https://polygonscan.com/address/0xB6Ac44d5A6388b8C7511F2fb20C0c03fD0CeDC83) |
| **USDC** (collateral) | `0x3c499c542cEF5E3811e1192ce70d8cC03d5c3359` | [View on Polygonscan](https://polygonscan.com/address/0x3c499c542cEF5E3811e1192ce70d8cC03d5c3359) |
| **ConditionalTokens** (Gnosis CTF) | read from `GET /account/wallet` → `conditional_tokens_address` | `https://polygonscan.com/address/{address}` |
| **CalibriResolver** (oracle) | _to be published_ — ask support | `https://polygonscan.com/address/{address}` |
| **AutoRedeemer** | read from `GET /account/wallet/auto-redeem` → `auto_redeemer_address` | `https://polygonscan.com/address/{address}` |
| **Your Safe** | read from `GET /account/wallet` → `proxy_address` | `https://polygonscan.com/address/{address}` |

The CTF Exchange was deployed on **2 August 2026** and carries Calibri's own
EIP-712 domain name — it is not a shared or third-party exchange. That domain
name is hashed into every order signature, which is why the app serves it rather
than hard-coding it, and why signatures made against a different exchange cannot
be replayed here.

<Note>
  **ConditionalTokens is not ours.** It is Gnosis' audited Conditional Token Framework, already deployed on Polygon and used by other prediction markets. Calibri deploys against the canonical instance rather than a fork — your collateral sits in a contract that predates us and is not under our control.
</Note>

## Wallet infrastructure

These four are what a **Safe** is made of. You never need them to trade, or to move funds
from a Safe that exists — they matter for exactly one case: a Safe address that was funded
before it was deployed. Deployment happens on your first deposit, so an address can hold
USDC with no contract at it yet, and both factories below are **permissionless**, meaning
anyone can deploy it. That is what keeps
[withdrawing without Calibri](/non-custodial/withdraw-without-calibri) true even for a
wallet we never got around to creating.

| Contract | Address | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Safe factory** | _to be published_ | Deploys your Safe at its derived address |
| **Safe singleton** | _to be published_ | The implementation every Safe proxy delegatecalls |
| **Safe fallback handler** | _to be published_ | Handles signature checks and other non-core calls |
| **WebAuthn signer factory** | _to be published_ | Deploys the secp256r1 signer contract a passkey owns |

<Note>
  The mainnet values are published here once the production contracts are deployed. Until then, ask support — they are fixed per network, identical for every member, and not secret: the Safe factory is readable from the exchange's own `safeFactory` view, and the singleton and handler from any deployed Safe's storage.
</Note>

## Reading the live values

Signed in, these two endpoints return the exact addresses your account is
trading against:

```
GET /api/v2/atlas/account/wallet
GET /api/v2/atlas/account/wallet/markets/:id
```

The member-level call returns `chain_id`, `blockchain_key`, `usdc_address`,
`conditional_tokens_address`, `exchange_address`, `domain_name`, your own
`proxy_address` (your Safe), and `owner_address` (what owns it). The per-market
call adds `condition_id`, `yes_token_id`, and `no_token_id` — the two ERC-1155
outcome tokens for that market.

<Warning>
  Read these addresses from the API rather than copying them into your own client. A wrong address silently sends signatures somewhere they cannot settle.
</Warning>

## Verifying it yourself

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your Safe holds your money">
    Take `proxy_address` from `GET /account/wallet` and open it on Polygonscan. Its USDC
    balance is your money, in a contract only you can authorise. Nothing about
    that depends on Calibri being honest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the exchange you are signing for">
    Confirm `exchange_address` from the API matches the CTF Exchange above, and
    that the contract has code at that address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the collateral">
    Confirm `usdc_address` is native USDC on Polygon — the address in the table
    above — and not a look-alike token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check a specific market">
    For any market, verify its `condition_id` and the `yes_token_id` /
    `no_token_id` against ConditionalTokens. Those token ids are derived from the
    resolver, the question and the collateral, so they name exactly one outcome
    of one market and never change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch a settlement land">
    After a market resolves, the redemption that pays you is a transaction on
    ConditionalTokens against your Safe. It is on the explorer like anything
    else.
  </Step>
</Steps>
