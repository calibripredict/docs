Welcome to Calibri — a full-stack prediction market engine built for traders, developers, and market makers who want programmatic access to real-world event markets. Calibri gives you a Central Limit Order Book (CLOB), real-time WebSocket streams, and REST APIs to trade, build, and analyze prediction markets at any scale. Whether you're placing your first order or wiring up an automated market-making strategy, this documentation has everything you need to get started.

## Get started in three steps

Follow the path below to go from zero to your first live trade on Calibri.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet and get API credentials">
    Sign an EIP-712 typed-data message with your Polygon wallet to prove ownership, then call `POST /auth/api-key` to receive your `apiKey`, `secret`, and `passphrase`. These credentials unlock all authenticated trading endpoints.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse markets">
    Call `GET /markets` on the Market API to discover active prediction markets, view current best bids and asks, and find the event you want to trade. Filter by category, status, or keyword to narrow the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place your first order">
    Submit a signed limit order to `POST /order` on the CLOB API. Calibri matches your order peer-to-peer against resting liquidity and settles the trade using fully collateralized ERC-1155 outcome tokens.
  </Step>
</Steps>
