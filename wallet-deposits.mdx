Calibri settles everything in **USDC**, a US-dollar stablecoin with **6 decimals**
(one base unit = `0.000001` USDC). Stakes, fees, winnings, and rewards are all
denominated in USDC.

## Funding your Safe

Your funds live in **your own on-chain Safe**. Funding is simply sending USDC to
its address:

- The Safe is a **1-of-1 Gnosis Safe** owned solely by you — by your wallet, or
  by the signer contract derived from your passkey. The operator deploys it and
  relays your signed orders, but **can never move funds without your signature**.
- You fund it by holding **USDC** in the Safe on the relevant network.
- As part of Safe setup, the Safe **pre-approves** the operator to pull the taker
  fee at settlement, up to a capped amount — so you only ever *sign*; the
  operator pays gas. See [The Safe](/non-custodial/the-safe) and
  [Balances](/non-custodial/balances) for details.

How you get USDC in depends on which wallet you use
([your options](/non-custodial/wallets)):

- **Passkey wallet** — send USDC straight to your Safe address, from an exchange
  or any wallet. There is nothing else to do, and you never pay gas.
- **Your own wallet** — move USDC from your wallet into your Safe in the app.
  That transfer comes from your own wallet, so it needs a small amount of the
  network's gas token (POL on Polygon). It is the only transaction you ever pay
  for — everything after it is relayed by the operator.

Because the funds are on-chain and controlled by your wallet, they remain
withdrawable and redeemable even if the operator is offline.

<Note>
  The live contract and token addresses for your network (USDC, the Conditional Token Framework, the exchange) are served by the app's CTF-config API — the app reads them per network rather than hard-coding them. See [Deployed addresses](/smart-contracts/deployed-addresses).
</Note>

## After funding

Once your Safe is funded you're ready to trade. Winning contracts pay **1.00 USDC per share** at
resolution, with **no fee at settlement** — your only trading cost is the taker
fee at the moment your order fills.
